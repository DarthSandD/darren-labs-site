Every Darren Labs system follows the same discipline — no claim without evidence.

  2. 02 GenerateAgents build the first version.

Flagship 01

VoltWorks Power

Power-system CAD automation that removes repetitive drafting — so engineers can design with confidence, and beginners can understand what they are looking at.

  • True-scale 3D substation — drawn at real heights, 1 unit = 1 m, with equipment you can inspect.
  • SLD builder with its own 3D view — draw a single-line diagram, see it in 3D, and check what changes when you open a breaker.
  • Calculators that explain themselves — guided onboarding and plain language, matched to industrial standards.
  • Built to standards — IEC 60364 / 60909, IEEE 1584-2018, NFPA 70E.

Architecture

Why two flagships prove the architecture.

VoltWorks is not a single tool. It is a system: agent orchestration to build it, verification loops to check it, and a coherent design that holds calculators, 3D, and diagrams together. AI Architecture Patterns is the other half: the thinking, tradeoffs, and judgment behind choosing the right system in the first place. That is what an AI architect does — design systems that can build real, usable software, again and again, and explain why.
