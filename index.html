Flagships, in order

Models first. Styx second. The rest, each on its own page.

Start with the models. Second is Styx, the desktop agent that runs fully locally. Below them: VoltWorks for power-system CAD, MEP Suits for building services, and AI Architecture Patterns, the notes from building both.

01

First flagship, AI Models, Live, Free

DarrenLabs AI Models

Two open Qwen models, renamed for DarrenLabs, with house rules that say what they are and what they must not invent. Ran on ai-lab in Batam. Timed in public, slow results included. 7B: 169 tokens per second on 32GB VRAM, on average. 32B: 54. Run both free, on your own machine, with Ollama.

Open the models page

02

Second flagship, Desktop agent, Fork, MIT

Styx

A desktop agent that runs fully locally, with Apple liquid glass and a local-only first run, so no remote VM is needed. A fork of Nous Research Hermes Agent, commit 25b5001, with the agent itself unchanged. Add your own provider key in Settings, then chat.

Open the Styx page

03

Flagship, Power

VoltWorks Power

Power-system CAD that removes repetitive drafting. True-scale 3D (1 unit = 1 m), a single-line-diagram (SLD) builder with an integrated 3D view, and engineering calculators that show their work.

IEC 60364 and 60909, IEEE 1584-2018, NFPA 70E

04

Flagship, Building Services

MEP Suits

A building-services mechanical toolkit, built by an engineer for the plant room. Eight calculators: Duct Sizer, Psychrometrics, Ventilation (ASHRAE 62.1), Pipe Hydraulics, Water and Coil Loads, Fan Laws, Room Cooling Load, and Unit Converter; plus a 3D System Viewer (outdoor AC unit, duct runs, industrial pipes) linked live to the duct and pipe calculators.

Every tool shows its method and formula, and checks results against ASHRAE and CIBSE design bands. This is a design aid: results must be verified by a licensed engineer before construction or procurement.

05

Flagship, Architecture

AI Architecture Patterns

A visual design library for AI systems: diagrams, trade-offs, runnable demos, and when not to use each pattern.
