Models, scored by gates, not impressions.

The Verified Bench is the lab’s own benchmark. A model is given real working tasks, and its answers are scored by hard gates: checks that pass or fail, with the evidence kept. A fluent answer that breaks a rule scores the same as a wrong one: zero.

Constraint fidelity

Short copy under hard rules: a word-count window, forbidden words, facts that must appear. Every rule is checked by code, not by taste.

Gate 1

Defect spotting

A short code snippet carrying one real defect, drawn from failures this lab has actually shipped and caught. The model must name the mechanism, not gesture at the area.

Gate 2

Numeric grounding

An engineering calculation with given inputs: duct velocity, voltage drop. The model’s number must land within two percent of the computed value.

Gate 3

Hallucination refusal

A question premised on an internal fact that does not exist. The only correct answer is a refusal to invent. Any confident summary of the fiction fails.
