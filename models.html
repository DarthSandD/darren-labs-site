Honest first

Here is the plain version.

We did not train these from scratch. The brains inside them come from Alibaba Qwen, and we say so on every page, because hiding the base would be the easiest way to lose trust. What we did do is real work: we put them on our own Oracle VM in Batam, gave them a DarrenLabs name and house rules, asked them a question, and timed the answer. Then we published the recipe so you can do the same on your own machine.

Speed, on 32GB VRAM

What changes when it gets a GPU.

Our VM has no GPU, so our own measured numbers are CPU numbers: 8.8 tokens per second for 7B, 1.89 for 32B. Those are slow, and they are ours. The numbers below are different: they are the average of published benchmarks for a 32GB VRAM card, the RTX 5090 class, running the same size of model. A 32B Q4 model is about 20GB, so it fits in 32GB with room left for context. We show the average, and we show how we got it, so you can check it.

How the average is made: 7B, (150 Ollama 8B + 185.9 llama.cpp 4K + 169.8 llama.cpp 8K) / 3 = 168.6, shown as 169. 32B, (45.1 Ollama 32B + 61.4 llama.cpp 4K + 55.5 llama.cpp 8K) / 3 = 54.0, shown as 54. Sources: Hardware Corner RTX 5090 llama.cpp benchmarks, and Databasemart RTX 5090 Ollama benchmarks. Context, quantisation, and engine change the number, so treat 169 and 54 as averages, not promises. When we run on a 32GB GPU ourselves, these averages will be replaced by our own measured numbers.

What that means in plain time: the same 292-token 32B test answer, 5.4 seconds of generation at 54 tokens per second, against 154.5 seconds of generation at 1.89 on CPU. That is about 29 times faster, and it is why the big model goes from offline-only on CPU to real chat on a GPU.

Run them free, anywhere

Take them home. You do not need us.

You need Ollama and the same recipe we used. No account, no endpoint, no fee, no waiting on our machine. Run it on yours, at your speed, and keep your notes to yourself.

# 7B
ollama pull qwen2.5:7b
ollama create darrenlabs-7b -f Modelfile.darrenlabs-7b

# 32B, give it time on CPU, use the Ollama API for first tests
ollama pull qwen3:32b
ollama create darrenlabs-32b -f Modelfile.darrenlabs-32b

Modelfiles and model cards will be published free, with no restriction beyond the base licence and attribution. Free and open here means the recipe, not a public chat box on our VM. A public box would get abused in a day, and on our CPU VM the big model still takes about 7 minutes per short answer. On a 32GB VRAM GPU, that same answer averages about 5.4 seconds of generation.

Use, and limits

Helpful, if you stay in charge.

Use them for

  • Turning a rough CAD, MEP, or power-engineering note into something tidy
  • Checklists, and a first pass at a drawing QA note
  • A first draft you will read, fix, and sign off yourself

Do not use them for, unchecked

  • A design decision on its own
  • A calculation nobody checked
  • Anything that goes onto a drawing or to a client without a human reading it first
